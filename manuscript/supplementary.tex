\section*{S1. Reproducibility}

All results are generated by the released code from the raw simulation records. The repository
(\url{https://github.com/kenoto99/v2g-ppo-two-area}, release v3.0.1; Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23851585)
contains \texttt{REPRODUCE.md} with the ordered commands, \texttt{results/ENVIRONMENT.txt} with the exact
package versions, \texttt{results/CHECKSUMS.sha256} with SHA-256 checksums of every raw result, table, figure,
trained policy and PDF, and \texttt{results/verification\_report.md}, the output of \texttt{scripts/verify\_all.py}.
That script re-derives every number in the manuscript independently of the script that builds the tables and
compares the two; it also re-runs the pandapower verification and the physics tests and checks that every
trained policy carries the final model version tag (\texttt{env\_version = v3}).

\paragraph{Random seeds.} PPO training seeds 0 to 4 (main), 0 to 2 (ablation, squashed policy), 0 and 1
(weight sensitivity). Training-day generator \texttt{default\_rng(1000\,seed + env)} for four parallel
environments; validation days \texttt{default\_rng(10000)}; test days \texttt{default\_rng(2026)}; stress days
\texttt{default\_rng(777)}; the disturbance of each hour \texttt{default\_rng([scenario seed, hour])}, identical
for every controller and treatment.

\paragraph{Package versions.} Python 3.13, NumPy 2.4.6, SciPy 1.18.1, PyTorch 2.15.0 (CUDA build, executed on CPU), Gymnasium 1.4.0,
Stable-Baselines3 2.9.2, pandapower 3.5.6, numba 0.68.1, matplotlib 3.11.2.

\section*{S3. Model verification details}

Base case of the IEEE 33-bus feeder: minimum voltage \ValVmin~p.u.\ at bus 18, loss \ValLoss~kW. Largest
voltage difference from pandapower over 20 random operating points: $\ValVerr$~p.u. Two-area LFC without
AGC after a 0.02-p.u.\ step: $\Delta f=\ValDfSim$~Hz (theory \ValDfTh), $\Delta P_{\mathrm{tie}}=\ValTieSim$~MW
(theory \ValTieTh). The physics tests in \texttt{tests/test\_physics.py} additionally verify the regulation-energy
integral against a closed-form case, the feasibility of random (including out-of-range) actions on 40 train and
stress days with the 60-s and the exact 3600-s fast layer, that no upward reserve is credited when driving alone
takes the fleet below its floor, and that raw-action clipping and physical projection are counted separately.

\section*{S6. Analyses added after the technical review of the revised manuscript}

Three analyses were added in response to the technical review of the revised manuscript. They reuse the
trained policies and the test-day scenarios of the main evaluation and add no new training run.
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{scripts/dao\_rho\_sweep.py} re-optimizes the day-ahead benchmark for reserve shares
$\rho\in\{0.0,0.1,\dots,0.6\}$ on the test and stress days and reports a per-day oracle
(Table~\ref{tab:daorho} of the manuscript). The $\rho=0.3$ row reproduces the DAO column of Table~\ref{tab:test}.
\item \texttt{scripts/activation\_powerflow.py} re-solves every test-day hour at $P^{\mathrm{set}}\pm R$ and at the
realized peak droop power of the simulated window and records voltages and branch currents
(Table~\ref{tab:activation}). The schedule case reproduces the violation hours of Table~\ref{tab:test}.
\item \texttt{scripts/chained\_days.py} runs the 30 test days back to back with the terminal fleet energy carried
into the next day (Table~\ref{tab:chained}). Day 1 reproduces the first independent test day exactly.
\end{itemize}
The verification script checks each of these consistency conditions and recomputes the corresponding macros.

\section*{S7. Remaining model assumptions}

Each area is represented by 25 identical copies of one IEEE 33-bus feeder; the fleet of each feeder is one
aggregated battery with a single SoC, so SoC dispersion and the reduced deliverable power of vehicles at
their limits are not represented; charger reactive power is not used; chargers are a first-order lag with
saturation, without current limits or control-loop dynamics; communication delays between the aggregator and
the chargers are not modeled; all chargers and vehicles are identical; LFC and fleet parameters are fixed at
representative values and were not varied in training or testing; the LFC model is linear with
representative parameters; load, PV and availability profiles are synthetic and shaped after published
measurements; hour-ahead forecasts are assumed exact; the terminal SoC requirement is a soft penalty, and consecutive-day
operation shows a downward drift of the residential fleet energy for every controller (Table~\ref{tab:chained}); the fast layer of each hour is a 60-s window containing
one step event and a fluctuating component, and the regulation energy of the remaining 59 minutes is obtained
from a second fluctuation-only window (checked against exact 3600-s simulations); the throughput term is a
linear proxy for degradation; the weights of the reward were chosen by the authors and their effect is shown
in the ablation and sensitivity studies.
